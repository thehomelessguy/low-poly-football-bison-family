# Reference and modeling notes

These are newly modeled, photo-based interpretations of the Buffalo Bills Family Circle bison family, not scans or the artist's original CAD files. The sculpture is **Legacy (2026)** by **Blessing Hancock**, fabricated by **ART Research Enterprises**.

## References inspected

- [Installation gallery supplied by the user](https://www.buffalobills.com/photos/photos-bison-installation-at-the-buffalo-bills-family-circle): front and elevated family views; photos 24 and 34 show especially useful side profiles of the cow/calf and bull.
- [Construction and design gallery](https://www.buffalobills.com/photos/photos-buffalo-bills-reveal-renders-progress-of-bison-statues-construction): workshop photos reveal the calf's proportions, rear leg angle, tail with angular tuft, and stripe construction. The actual installation photos take precedence over the early renderings.
- [Lighting-test gallery](https://www.buffalobills.com/photos/photos-bison-statue-lighting-test): close side views show the eye and nostril shapes, and the bowed, concave front cap of the outlined charge stripe.
- [Official art collection / artist credit](https://www.buffalobills.com/stadium/art-collection/).
- [Official dimensions announcement](https://www.buffalobills.com/news/buffalo-bills-reveal-renders-of-bison-statues-at-highmark-stadium-s-family-circle): stated lengths of 29, 23 and 13 feet inform the relative model lengths. Heights and widths in the recreations are estimated from photographs, not measured survey data.

## Features modeled

Revision 3 shortens the bull's swept lower foreleg and gives it a separate faceted hoof with a sloping sole. Thin stripe rails, horn tips and the calf's tail stem are reinforced for small-scale printing. The calf's stripe rails are also widened in the plane of the outline. These are printing adaptations rather than measured dimensions of the original metalwork.

Revision 2 replaces the first model's ring-based body surfaces with irregular triangulated silhouettes and manually placed head landmarks. The adult forehead rises above a tucked muzzle; shoulder width, chest depth and back taper were revised. The bull's lifted-leg side and joint positions, cow's head turn and calf's rear-leg spread were adjusted using the additional views. Reference photography has perspective distortion and incomplete coverage, so exact panel boundaries and joint locations remain estimates.

- Three individually shaped animals; calf has a shorter, raised face, smaller hump and broad ears without adult horns.
- Bull has one lifted front leg. Cow and calf have staggered standing poses.
- Tall front shoulders, descending back, slimmer hindquarters, angular leg joints, beard on the adults, faceted horns and tail tuft.
- Recessed diamond-like eyes and nostrils, plus open charge outlines on both flanks with integrated attachment posts.
- Flat hoof contact surfaces and closed meshes suitable for slicing.

## Deliberate printing adaptations

Stripe rails, standoffs, horn tips, calf legs and tail stems are thickened for small-scale printing. Stripe posts are fused to the body. Small sealed internal pockets at intersections are filled. Separate metal panel seams, rivets, wiring, lighting and smoke mechanisms are omitted. The facet layout, concealed back surfaces, exact poses and underside details remain approximations. The optional bases are original display accessories, not measured replicas of the stadium plinth.

Local reference images are kept in `references/` for this modeling project and are not included in the deliverable ZIP. Photo copyrights remain with their respective photographers / Buffalo Bills.
